Salmon and Scrambled Eggs on Toast

Description

A quick and easy delicious breakfast to start your day.

Ready to eat in under 30 minutes.

Ingredients:

  • 1tbsp butter
  • 2 large free range eggs
  • 1tbsp milk
  • 1 slice wholemeal bread
  • 2 slices smoked salmon
  • Salt and freshly ground pepper

Steps:

  1. Melt the butter in a saucepan over a low heat.
  2. Add the eggs and stir continuously for 5 minutes.
  3. Once the eggs are thick, add the milk.
  4. Remove pan from the heat and season with the salt and pepper.
  5. Spoon the scrambles eggs onto the buttered toast and place the salmon ontop.
