Thai Green Curry

Description

A fragrant and creamy Thai green chicken curry dish, quick and easy, yet, delicious.

With just 10 minutes prep time and 20 minutes cooking time,this dish will ready to impress in only 30 minutes.

Ingedients:

  • 22g new potatos, cut into chunks
  • 100g green beans, trimmed and halved
  • 1tbsp vegetable or sunflower oil
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1tbsp Thai green curry paste
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 2tsp Thai fish sauce
  • 1tsp caster sugar
  • 400g diced boneless chicken
  • 2 lime leaves
  • Handful basil leaves
  • Boiled rice

Steps:

  1. Boil the potato chunks in a pan for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the chopped and halved green beans to the boiling water and cook for a further 3 minutes. Both the green beans and potatos should be tender and not too soft.
  3. Drain the pan and put to one side.
  4. Heat the oil in a large frying pan or wok over a high heat until very hot. Add the chopped garlic and cook for a few seconds until golden.
  5. Add Thai green curry paste, stirring for a few seconds to realease the flavours.
  6. Pour in the coconut milk, letting it come to the bubble.
  7. Stir in the Thai fish sauce and caster sugar, followed by the diced boneless chicken. Turn the heat down to a simmer, cover, and let cook for about 8 minutes until the chicken is cooked.
  8. Tip in the potatos and beans, letting them warm through in the hot coconut milk. Then add the finely shredded lime leaves.
  9. Add the handful of basil leaves, leaving them only briefly on the heat to avoid losing their colour and brightness.
  10. Scatter with lime to garnish and serve with boiled rice.
