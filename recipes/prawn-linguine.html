Tomato, Chilli & Prawn Linguine

Description

A super simple, quick and easy recipe combining sweet tomatoes and subtle chilli to complement the succulent prawns, followed by a little lemon zest to top things off.

In just 5 minutes of preperation, and 15 minutes cook time, this dish will be ready to enjoy in only 20 minutes.

Ingredients:

  • 300g dried linguine
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 400g cherry vine tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 large chilli, chopped and deseeded
  • 3 cloves garlic, finely sliced
  • 150g raw king prawns
  • 1/2 lemon, squeezed
  • 1/2 25g pack of basil, leaves torn

Steps:

  1. Heat a large pan of salted water and bring to the boil
  2. While waiting for the pan to boil, heat the olive oil in large frying over a high heat
  3. Add the pasta to the boiling water and cook for 10-12 minutes
  4. After adding the pasta to the boiling water, add the chopped tomatoes to the frying pan, cook for 3 minutes until they begin to blister
  5. Now turn the heat down slightly and add the chopped chilli and garlic to the frying pan. Continue to cook for 3-4 minutes until fragrant
  6. Add the king prawns to the frying pan. Cook for 1-2 minutes until they turn solid pink
  7. Drain the linguine, but keep a cup of cooking water in a cup to add to the sauce
  8. Pour the linguine directly into the frying pan and mix with enough cooking water to coat linguine
  9. Season the linguine with 1/2 the torn basil and squeeze lemon juice while stirring
  10. Once fully mixed, add the rest of the basil and serve
